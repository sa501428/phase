How to interpret this view

The graph is assembled from quality-eligible local read sequences, including soft clips. Unitigs are compacted sequence segments. Arrows record observed k-mer adjacency; loops can represent repeats or duplications.

Suggested haplotypes are compatible, fragment-threaded allele patterns within connected local evidence blocks. Missing sequence is unknown. A contradictory SNP vetoes grouping, even below the support threshold.

These are local sequence hypotheses, not a measured number of cancer cell clones. Disconnected blocks, repeats, errors, allele-specific coverage, copy number and PCR bias limit inference. A graph path without read linkage is not counted as a clone.

Cis requires alleles observed on the same physical fragment. Separate allele patterns are compatible with trans, but do not establish trans in a mixture with unrestricted ploidy.

SA tags, supplementary alignments and mate metadata provide inversion/translocation clues. Partner sequence is not assembled remotely. Some complex topology will remain unresolved.

No upload, telemetry, persistence or downsampling. Limits fail explicitly: 200 kb region, 100,000 records, 2 million bases, 200,000 oriented k-mers, 400,000 edges. Same-contig mate lookup is indexed and bounded; off-contig partners remain metadata. Clear session releases case data.

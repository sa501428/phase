Supported formats & coordinate rules

All input coordinates are 1-based. Accepted: chr:posREF>ALT, chr:pos:REF:ALT, CHR POS REF ALT, VCF, headed CSV/TSV and MAF, plus exact DNA g. and transcript c. substitutions, deletions, insertions, duplications and delins. VCF multi-allelic rows are split. Identical normalized alleles are counted once.

Use an explicit transcript accession for coding HGVS. Ambiguous gene/transcript mappings, protein-only annotations, rs IDs, symbolic alleles and uncertain coordinates are flagged. Choose the assembly that matches your BAM; chromosome names or lengths cannot verify its assembly. Tables with a header should be pasted as a single table.

No reference genome is required. Supplied REF alleles are assumed, not independently validated. Indels are assessed at the given CIGAR coordinates; equivalent shifted events in repeats may be missed. For HGVS deletions without sequence, “REF” means an intact observed span. Sequence-free duplications require the copied source and insertion to be observed on the same alignment.

Evidence settings

Alleles below this threshold are hidden and excluded from phase matching, clashes and helper links. Exactly 2% is included. Set 0 to disable suppression, then rerun.

Secondary, supplementary, QC-failed and unmapped records are excluded. Missing base quality and MAPQ 255 are unknown. Overlapping mates vote once; disagreements are excluded.

Search around and between targets for biallelic SNP markers that can connect phase blocks. Helpers are always highlighted in purple.

Helper support uses distinct fragment coordinates plus RX/UB tags when present, with duplicate-flagged records excluded. This is a conservative molecule proxy, not UMI consensus. Without a genome, the modal base is the baseline; the nonmodal allele fraction must exceed your threshold. Both alleles need minimum support. Each link also needs support for both phase patterns.

FRAGMENT-LEVEL OBSERVATIONS

Phase evidence

Together frequency = both ALT ÷ (both ALT + A-only + B-only). Separate frequency = (A-only + B-only) ÷ the same denominator. Only fragments callable as REF/ALT at both sites enter these frequencies. Neither-ALT, other alleles and conflicts are reported separately. “Separate” describes observed patterns; it does not establish trans phase in a tumor.

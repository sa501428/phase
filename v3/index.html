Selection & interpretation

Click a read or connector to toggle its whole pair. Shift-click selects a range from top to bottom and left to right, including across scrolling. Shift-drag selects pairs in a rectangular area. Drag horizontally to pan; wheel scrolls rows; Ctrl/Command+wheel zooms. Click the coordinate/reference track to select a site, then sort pairs by that allele.

Merge creates your named haplotype. Contradictory callable bases/deletions or insertions block a merge. Missing coverage is unknown. Soft clips remain visible unaligned evidence and do not vote as reference-aligned bases.

Use Hide for unhelpful pairs, Restore hidden or Undo to bring them back. The checkbox above controls whether hidden pairs contribute to consensus and VAF.

Hierarchy compares jointly observed SNPs in your constructed groups. Similarity is not an inferred tumor ancestry. Group share counts assigned pairs; site VAF counts alternate alleles among callable fragments. Neither is a cancer-cell fraction.

Secondary/QC-failed/unavailable sequences and MAPQ 255 do not vote. Missing base quality is accepted as unknown. Duplicate-flagged pairs are filtered from the pileup and evidence by default. Show duplicates restores them; there is no UMI consensus.

No upload, telemetry, browser storage or downsampling. Bounded local reads only: 200 kb target, 100,000 fetched records, two million read bases. Off-contig mates remain metadata. Clear session releases case data.
